OUR APPROACH

SMC Analysis Technology

BOS / CHoCH

Study breaks of structure and potential changes in market character.

💧

Liquidity

Fair Value Gap

Learn to recognize price imbalances and assess them in context.

🕒

Multi-Timeframe

Compare higher-timeframe context with lower-timeframe setups.

Analysis Workflow

  • Start with higher-timeframe market structure.
  • Mark meaningful highs, lows and liquidity areas.
  • Wait for confirmation; avoid chasing price.
  • Define invalidation and risk before entry.

Risk Management

  • Use a predefined risk limit.
  • Never assume a setup must work.
  • Avoid risking money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Keep a journal and review decisions.
LEARN THE BASICS

SMC Education

Learn key concepts with these two video lessons and quick summaries.

01 · Market Structure — BOS & CHoCH

Study swing highs and lows, break of structure, and possible change of character. Wait for confirmation instead of assuming every break is a trade signal.

If the video is unavailable in your region, open YouTube and search “SMC BOS CHoCH market structure tutorial”.

02 · Liquidity & Fair Value Gaps

Learn to mark prior highs and lows, understand liquidity sweeps, and study imbalances alongside the higher-timeframe trend and risk plan.

If the video is unavailable, search YouTube for “SMC liquidity sweep fair value gap tutorial”.
